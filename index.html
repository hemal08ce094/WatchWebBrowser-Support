Watch Web Browser

Help for the Apple Watch app and its iPhone companion.

Most questions are answered below. If yours isn't, email us.

Getting started

What do I need?

An Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later, paired with an iPhone on iOS 17 or later. Install the app on your iPhone and the watch app installs with it (or add it from the App Store on the watch).

How do I open a website or search?

On the watch, tap Search and type or dictate a web address or a few words. An address opens the site; anything else runs a search with the search engine you picked in Settings (Google, DuckDuckGo or Bing).

How do I add favourites?

Add them on the watch, or in the iPhone app with Add. Favourites added on the iPhone sync to the watch. There is no limit on how many you can save.

Reading and controls

What is Reader view?

Reader view shows the article text of a page on its own, without menus or ads. Swipe right on a favourite, a top site or a recent page, or use the Just opened card on the home screen. Turn the Digital Crown to scroll and choose from five text sizes.

Some pages, such as home pages and link lists, have no article to show. Reader view then offers Open full page instead.

Can I change the text size on the full web page?

Full pages open in Apple's own web view on the watch, which the app can't restyle. For larger text, use Reader view, or turn up Text Size in the watch's Settings app.

Watch face, Siri and Shortcuts

How do I add it to my watch face or Smart Stack?

Touch and hold the watch face, tap Edit, choose a complication slot and pick Watch Web Browser. There are two: Search, which opens the keyboard straight away, and Top Favorite, which opens your first favourite. Both can also be added to the Smart Stack.

Which Shortcuts actions are there?

On the watch: Open Favorite and Search the Web. On the iPhone: Open Favorite, which opens the site in Safari. Find them in the Shortcuts app, or ask Siri using the app's name, for example "Search Watch Web Browser".

Watch and iPhone together

How do I continue a page on my iPhone?

While a page is open on the watch, your nearby iPhone offers it through Handoff. In Reader view you can also tap Send to iPhone; the page appears under From your watch in the iPhone app and opens in Safari.

This sends the page you opened. Links you followed inside that page stay on the watch.

My favourites don't appear on the watch
  • Open the app on both the iPhone and the watch once, with the watch unlocked and near the iPhone.
  • Check that Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are on and the watch shows as connected in the Watch app on the iPhone.
  • Add or edit a favourite on the iPhone to send the list again.

Purchases and privacy

Is anything locked behind a purchase?

No. Watch Web Browser is a paid app and every feature is included. If you bought the earlier Pro unlock, nothing changes for you.

How do I clear my history?

On the watch, open History and tap Clear History. This also resets Top Sites.
